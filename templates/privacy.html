Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Spendly personal expense tracking application (the "Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, as well as data generated through your use of the app:

  • Account details, such as your name and email address
  • Login credentials, stored in encrypted form
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as when you log in and which features you use

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve the Service, including to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Record, display, and summarize your expenses
  • Diagnose technical issues and keep the Service secure
  • Communicate with you about your account or the Service

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure, such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at support@spendly.app.
